OUR STORY

IT STARTED WITH A LOVE FOR SPORT.

COURTLY was imagined as more than just a place to play. We wanted to create a welcoming sports destination where players could enjoy quality facilities, discover new challenges and become part of an active community.

Too often, passionate badminton and table tennis players had to settle for crowded halls, inconsistent lighting, or complicated booking calls. We set out to change that by combining tournament-grade surfaces with a seamless digital reservation experience.

From early-morning singles drills to vibrant weekend doubles leagues, every detail of our arena is crafted so players of all backgrounds feel inspired the moment they step onto the court.
