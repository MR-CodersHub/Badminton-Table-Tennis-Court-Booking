    A FEW THINGS YOU MAY WANT TO KNOW.

    Each membership includes a specific number of court booking hours and access benefits based on the selected plan.

    Members can explore the available badminton and table tennis courts, subject to court availability and applicable booking policies.

    Starter includes 4 court booking hours and standard booking access, while Pro Athlete includes 10 hours and priority booking access along with its listed benefits.

    Elite Player includes 20 court booking hours, premium booking access, special tournament benefits, and exclusive member privileges.

    Court booking availability and applicable rates follow the existing website booking policy, with hourly bookings available directly on the Court page.

    Members can access their player dashboard to view relevant membership information, subject to the functionality implemented in the project.

    Membership changes follow the club's membership policy and can be reviewed with the club team based on your account status.
